== Divergence register

Every known cross-adapter divergence from the
link:../_references/processing-model[processing model] is registered
here. Contract:

* A divergence is either **fixed**, **loud** (a named raise), or
  **SPEC'D** (documented here and pinned with a skip in the
  consistency suite, citing the entry ID).
* Consistency-suite exemptions must cite a register ID. An
  undocumented divergence is a bug — report it against this register.

[cols="2,4,6"]
|===
| ID | Adapter(s) | Divergence

| D-02
| oga
| Raw CR preserved in text content — §2.11 EOL normalization not
applied at parse. Decided permanent: normalization belongs at the
engine's parse layer; a facade read arm would only cover the read
verbs it touches and change content/source-position consistency.
Pinned in `spec/consistency/eol_normalization_spec.rb`.

| D-03
| ox, headed_ox
| `&#13;` character references collapse to LF. Decided permanent:
the reference-vs-literal distinction is lost at parse, so no facade
read can recover it. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/eol_normalization_spec.rb`.

| D-05
| ox, headed_ox
| PI content is dropped on parse for element-child PIs, XPath-minted
wrappers, and quoted data (some document-level shapes retain it).
Pinned in `spec/moxml/c14n/adapter_delegation_spec.rb` and the
taxonomy differential.

| D-06
| ox, headed_ox
| Comment content padding stripped at parse. Pinned in
`spec/moxml/c14n/subset_spec.rb`.

| D-09
| oga, ox, headed_ox, libxml, leptris
| Element-level `to_xml(indent:)` is not supported by these engines:
the facade raises the named `Moxml::AdapterError` instead of
answering unindented output; `indent: 0` and the no-option path stay
common. The engine-level `serialize(indent:)` lane (direct
`Adapter.serialize`) still ignores or partially applies the knob.
Pinned in `spec/moxml/element_indent_spec.rb` and
`spec/moxml/adapter/shared_examples/adapter_contract.rb`.

| D-10
| ox, headed_ox
| SAX reports no separate CDATA/comment/processing-instruction
events. A handler that subscribes to one of those families gets the
named `Moxml::NotImplementedError` — loud, never a silently
incomplete stream. Text-only handlers still receive CDATA folded
into character data as the engine delivers it. Pinned in
`spec/moxml/sax_spec.rb`.

| D-11
| oga
| SAX parse is more lenient with malformed input than the model's
error authority. Decided permanent: the engine owns error reporting,
and pre-validating with a strict parse would defeat the streaming
lane. Pinned in `spec/moxml/sax_spec.rb`.

| D-13
| ox, headed_ox
| Inter-element whitespace-only text nodes are dropped at parse
(node counts and leading/trailing text differ from the other
adapters). Surfaced by the taxonomy differential. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/differential_taxonomy_spec.rb`.

| D-14
| oga
| Processing-instruction content leaks into text reads (oga's own
`text` folds PIs; the model says only text nodes contribute).
Surfaced by the taxonomy differential. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/differential_taxonomy_spec.rb`.

| D-15
| rexml
| The whitespace text node between a pre-root PI and the root is
retained (other adapters drop it). Surfaced by the taxonomy
differential. Pinned in `spec/consistency/differential_taxonomy_spec.rb`.

| D-16
| rexml
| Entity-expansion guard caps huge documents (~10k expansions) and
raised a bare engine error — the facade now names it a ParseError;
the cap itself remains (nokogiri's HUGE lane has no such cap).
Surfaced by the taxonomy differential. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/differential_taxonomy_spec.rb`.

| D-17
| oga
| The strict parse lane does not validate nesting well-formedness
(oga's strict is lexer-strict, not well-formedness-strict); bad
nesting parses where other adapters raise. Surfaced by the taxonomy
differential. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/differential_taxonomy_spec.rb`.

| D-18
| leptris, oga, ox, headed_ox
| Strict parse accepts invalid UTF-8 byte sequences (no encoding
validation at parse; the bytes flow through as binary). The
validating adapters (nokogiri, rexml, libxml) raise the named
ParseError. On leptris the facade raises the named ParseError in the
strict lane (default-UTF-8 rule; prolog-declared non-UTF-8 encodings
own their validity) pending engine-side validation —
leptris-ruby#514. Surfaced by the taxonomy differential. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/differential_taxonomy_spec.rb`.

| D-19
| nokogiri, libxml
| Adjacent text-like leaves (CDATA next to CDATA or text) coalesce
into a single text node on the libxml2 lane; oga, rexml, ox,
headed_ox, and leptris keep them as distinct nodes. The `text`
answer is identical — the node count and kinds differ. The model is
silent on text-node coalescing (an open decision). Surfaced by the
generated metamorphic differential (M5). Pinned in
`spec/consistency/metamorphic_properties_spec.rb` through
`InputGenerator::FEATURE_EXEMPT`.

| D-20
| rexml
| Trailing whitespace in processing-instruction data is dropped at
parse (the engine's instruction content is trimmed); leading
separator runs collapse per the model. Pinned in the taxonomy
differential (`pi trailing whitespace`, face `pi_content`).
|===
