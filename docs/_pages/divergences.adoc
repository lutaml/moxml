== Divergence register

Every known cross-adapter divergence from the
link:../_references/processing-model[processing model] is registered
here. Contract:

* A divergence is either **fixed**, **loud** (a named raise), or
  **SPEC'D** (documented here and pinned with a skip in the
  consistency suite, citing the entry ID).
* Consistency-suite exemptions must cite a register ID. An
  undocumented divergence is a bug — report it against this register.

[cols="2,4,6"]
|===
| ID | Adapter(s) | Divergence

| D-01
| oga
| The `xmlns:` default-namespace XPath idiom is unsupported by oga's
own engine (explicit bindings work). Pinned in
`spec/consistency/xpath_idioms_spec.rb`.

| D-02
| oga
| Raw CR preserved in text content — §2.11 EOL normalization not
applied at parse. Pinned in `spec/consistency/eol_normalization_spec.rb`.

| D-03
| ox, headed_ox
| `&#13;` character references collapse to LF. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/eol_normalization_spec.rb`.

| D-04
| oga

separator space. Pinned in `spec/moxml/c14n/adapter_delegation_spec.rb`.

| D-05
| ox, headed_ox
| PI content dropped at parse. Pinned in
`spec/moxml/c14n/adapter_delegation_spec.rb`.

| D-06
| ox, headed_ox

| D-07
