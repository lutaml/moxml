== Divergence register

Every known cross-adapter divergence from the
link:../_references/processing-model[processing model] is registered
here. Contract:

* A divergence is either **fixed**, **loud** (a named raise), or
  **SPEC'D** (documented here and pinned with a skip in the
  consistency suite, citing the entry ID).
* Consistency-suite exemptions must cite a register ID. An
  undocumented divergence is a bug — report it against this register.

[cols="2,4,6"]
|===
| ID | Adapter(s) | Divergence

| D-01
| oga
| The `xmlns:` default-namespace XPath idiom is unsupported by oga's
own engine (explicit bindings work). Pinned in
`spec/consistency/xpath_idioms_spec.rb`.

| D-02
| oga
| Raw CR preserved in text content — §2.11 EOL normalization not
applied at parse. Pinned in `spec/consistency/eol_normalization_spec.rb`.

| D-03
| ox, headed_ox
| `&#13;` character references collapse to LF. Pinned in
`spec/consistency/eol_normalization_spec.rb`.

| D-04
| oga
| The XML declaration surfaces as a `<?xml?>` PI; PI content keeps its
separator space. Pinned in `spec/moxml/c14n/adapter_delegation_spec.rb`.

| D-05
| ox, headed_ox
| PI content dropped at parse. Pinned in
`spec/moxml/c14n/adapter_delegation_spec.rb`.

| D-06
| ox, headed_ox
| Comment content padding stripped at parse. Pinned in
`spec/moxml/c14n/subset_spec.rb`.

| D-07
| rexml
| `text()` node tests return no text nodes (adapter XPath capability
gap, both axes). Pinned in `spec/moxml/c14n/subset_spec.rb`.

| D-08
| c14n subsystem
| Document-level PI/comment rendering not yet implemented (all
adapters; a subsystem TODO, not an engine divergence). Tracked in
`TODO.c14n/09-comments-pis-tests.md`.

| D-09
| oga, ox, headed_ox, libxml, leptris
| Element-level `to_xml(indent:)` silently ignored (libxml emits a
partial, unindented newline form). Document-level HTML/XHTML
indentation is honored on leptris/nokogiri and raises on incapable
engines. Pinned in
`spec/moxml/adapter/shared_examples/adapter_contract.rb`.

| D-10
| ox, headed_ox
| SAX emits no separate CDATA/comment/processing-instruction events
(sequences fold into text or are dropped). Pinned in
`spec/moxml/sax_spec.rb`.

| D-11
| oga
| SAX parse is more lenient with malformed input than the model's
error authority. Pinned in `spec/moxml/sax_spec.rb`.

| D-12
| ox, headed_ox
| The XPath translation covers a subset: no positional predicates,
attribute-value predicates, namespace/default-namespace tests,
`position()`/`count()`, `text()`, boolean expressions, `child::`/
`descendant::` axes, or unions. Pinned in
`spec/moxml/xpath_capabilities_spec.rb` and `spec/moxml/node_spec.rb`.
|===
